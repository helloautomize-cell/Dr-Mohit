## NMC and advertising rules (must follow)

- **No superlatives or guarantees.** Do not use "best", "No. 1", "first-ever", "100% accurate", "painless", "zero infection", "guaranteed" or "completely safe". The old website used several of these; do not carry them over.
- **No patient testimonials,** no patient success stories, no before/after images, and no identifiable patient faces without written consent.
- **No fee discounts or offers.**
- Doctor credentials must be accurate, and the registration number shown where available.
- Illustrations are labelled "Illustration only" where they could be mistaken for clinical results. This is already done in the prototype.
- AI-generated images of the real doctors may be used **only with each doctor's written consent and approval** of the final image.
