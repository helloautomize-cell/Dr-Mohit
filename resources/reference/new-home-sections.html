Mapping your knee

During surgery the system records the shape and alignment of your bones, without a separate CT scan.

A camera follows the knee as it bends, so the space on each side can be balanced before any bone is cut.

Our story

Four decades on Power House Road.

1980

A clinic opens

Dr. Amrit Gupta starts an orthopaedic practice on Power House Road, bringing bone and joint surgery closer to families in Malwa.

2000

Care for the mind

Dr. Amit Gupta joins as psychiatrist and the de-addiction centre begins.

2019

Ayushman Bharat

The hospital is empanelled under PM-JAY, so eligible card holders can be treated here.

2024

Robotic-assisted knees

The J&J VELYS™ robotic-assisted knee system is launched at the hospital’s Ortho Robotic Centre.

2025

Recognised by The Tribune
