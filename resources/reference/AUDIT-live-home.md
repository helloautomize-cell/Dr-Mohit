# Live Home audit (dr-mohit.vercel.app, from the 14 Nov screen recording)

The recording starts just below the hero, so the hero notes come from the current hero setup (one photo of Dr. Mohit with a caption row).

## Hero
- It shows one doctor for a hospital with three departments, so Eyes and Mind are invisible until the visitor scrolls.
- One static photo and a caption row: nothing moves or invites a click, which is why it reads as plain rather than premium.
- No proof near the top (years, PM-JAY, award), although the page has all three further down.
- The new hero fixes all three: three doctor panels that open one after another, a short three-line promise, and the facts row with the calculated 46 years.

## Across the page
- Large empty gaps between several sections (after the waterfall, before Our story, before the press and before the FAQ). One consistent section padding fixes it.
- Too many sections (13 plus hero) with two pairs of repeats. The new order has 12 plus hero.
